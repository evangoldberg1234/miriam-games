For parents

Brain Breaks are short learning pauses instead of ads. At the end of every level, whether Miriam finishes it or starts it over, she or Joyce answers a few questions: math, word problems, English, Russian, and Hebrew words, and the weekly Torah portion. Questions get a little harder as the game level goes up. Each child has her own level, saved on this iPad. They can switch players on the break screen.

The 💬 bubble lets Miriam chat with Mirlil, her Grok Bot. It asks for the family code once on this iPad, and only Mirlil can answer.
