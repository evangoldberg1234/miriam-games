Made with 💜 for Miriam

For parents

Brain Breaks are short learning pauses instead of ads. At the end of every level, whether Miriam finishes it or starts it over, she answers a few questions. Questions get a little harder as the game level goes up. Levels are saved on this iPad. Players can switch on the break screen.

The 💬 bubble lets Miriam chat with Mirlil. It asks for the family code once on this iPad. Stars can be earned in Practice and spent on hints. A new game costs 20 stars.

Treasure Map finds a level for the subjects that are turned on. Those levels are saved for Brain Breaks. She can quit and come back, or retake a subject anytime. It starts at the upper end of 1st grade, then adapts.

Book Club is for a book she finished. She picks the book, then tells Mirlil about it in the chat. A long book can earn 20 stars. A short book does not.

Homework is a photo of one worksheet. The check can take about a minute. It can award stars, up to 10 for a sheet and 20 homework stars a day. Hints never include the answer.
